# Autonomy roadmap

Working list, one item at a time, simplest first (details and reasoning: [PLAN.md](PLAN.md#autonomy-roadmap-2026-11-03-replaces-the-dog-companion-goal)).

- [x] 1. **Measured moves** — "forward N cm", "turn X°" from timed drives; distance/position counter (estimate). *Done 2026-11-03: forward 25 cm/s, reverse 43 cm/s, full-left radius 132.5 cm (2 s arc: 70 cm forward, 20 cm left → 32°); turns are timed from the arc run itself. Accuracy to check in use (forward speed looked low next to the arc run's ~37 cm/s). Turns need ~2 m of arc for 90°.*
- [ ] 2. **Snapshot task v0** — "take a picture of my dog" when he's in view: find, centre, approach, save the best frame to a gallery.
- [x] 3. **Gyro heading**: the M5StickS3 on the rover streams heading over Wi-Fi; turns by angle, heading hold on straight moves. *Priority raised 2026-11-03: the front wheels have play, so the rover drifts randomly on straight runs. Centre trim only fixes the steady part; heading-hold steering fixes the rest. Built 2026-11-03 ([firmware/m5stick-imu](firmware/m5stick-imu/README.md), `drive/imu.py`, Heading card on the dashboard). Mounted, and the angle reads true by hand. First road run: the 1 m straight held its average heading but zigzagged ±4° and ended 7° off. Short full-lock taps still felt zig-zaggy: a single steer key kept the wheels turned for 400 ms. Rover app 0.9 adds fine steering from back-to-back keys: `W W` centres the wheels now, `W W A/D` steers at 30% of full lock. Logged run: gentle left on every step still drifted right 4°, because the wheel play absorbs a 15° servo move. Heading hold now works out a steering amount (proportional to the error plus a slowly built trim for a steady pull) and blends straight, gentle and full-lock steps to reach it. Turns: 86° asked 90° → turn lead cut from 0.4 s to 0.15 s. **Done 2026-11-03:** 1 m straight ends 1.1° off (heading within ±2.4° all the way, was 7° off and zigzagging); 90° left turn measures 89.8°. The 90° turn reached +62° and then got stuck; a turn now stops if the heading stays unchanged for 1 s. To do: rerun both.*
- [ ] 3b. **Camera obstacles**: a depth AI model (Depth Anything V2) on the camera feed finds free floor and obstacles in the left/centre/right thirds, with distances from floor geometry. *Started 2026-11-03 (`mac/depth_service.py`, Obstacles card): 16 fps / 55 ms on the Mac GPU; first wander drove 1.2 m with the centre clear. Sectors are smoothed over 5 frames after a one-frame glitch. First mapping run (2026-11-04, `mac/explore.py` + `mac/floor_map.py`): 9 steps, about 2.6 m straight along the cabinets, mapped about 6.8 m² of floor; the scale check said distances read 2.4× too far, so the scale was corrected (bottom edge 20 → 8.5 cm). The run ended on a turn that barely turned, because the steering axle was glued. Added since: YOLO11s watches for dogs, cats and people (no driving while one is in view), a guard that stops mid-move, and stuck-steering detection. Second run (2026-11-04): 19 steps, about 5 m, 12 m² mapped, waited once for a person; it then drove into the balcony blinds, which the depth model read as far away. The cause: the horizon was taken from the farthest thing in view, so facing the blinds the floor read clear up to contact. The fix measures the camera geometry from that run (horizon at 0.43 of the height, distance = 3991/(row − horizon), rms 3.9 px over 18 frames) and treats "farther than the floor" below the horizon as not floor. Replayed on the same frames it reads 62/42/21 cm at a true 73/48/22 cm and would have stopped about 1.4 m out. The ToF/ultrasonic sensor will be the safety backstop and scale check.*
- [ ] 4. **Front distance** — ToF sensor (VL53L1X, ~US$8); stop before obstacles, wall following.
- [ ] 5. **Wheel odometry** — printed slotted disc + IR interrupter (~US$3); real distance travelled.
- [ ] 6. **Dead-reckoning pose** — gyro + odometry → x, y, heading; live trail on the dashboard.
- [ ] 7. **Landmarks** — printed AprilTags at known spots correct the drift: the rover knows where it is.
- [ ] 8. **Apartment map** — floor plan as a grid with named rooms; rover shown live on it.
- [ ] 9. **Go to a place** — "go to the kitchen": path planning with distance-sensor safety; return home.
- [ ] 10. **Tasks on the map** — "find the dog and take a picture": visit rooms, look, photograph, come back.
- [ ] 11. **Builds the map itself** — exploration; camera-only visual SLAM as an experiment.
- [ ] 12. **Docking and unattended runs** — return to a charger, scheduled tasks.
- [ ] 12b. **Apartment capture for simulation** (idea, 2026-11-03): whenever the rover drives, save frames with their pose (heading, odometer x/y), depth and time into a local dataset. Later, rebuild the apartment from them (photogrammetry or Gaussian splatting → mesh) as a simulation scene (Gazebo / Isaac Sim / MuJoCo) to train other robots. Needs good poses: items 6–7, or the phone/LiDAR path. *Recorder built 2026-11-03 (`mac/capture.py`, Record switch); not used on a drive yet.*
- [ ] 12c. **Apartment simulator** (2026-11-04): MuJoCo copy of the apartment from the floor plan + photos, edited on the dashboard Sim page (furniture, virtual doors), the real drive server/explorer running against it, batch runs scored from the truth. Found and fixed: a zero-frame-age bug, runs ending on near-complete turns, and rear-corner collisions in reverse arcs (now 0 collisions in 8 runs / 101 m). Next: perception on real photos, the ultrasonic sensor, then ROS 2.
- [ ] 13. **Full stack** — 2D LiDAR, Raspberry Pi onboard, ROS 2 + slam_toolbox + Nav2, Tailscale.

Later add-on: the owner's WaveSight home-sensing project as a room-level position hint (see PLAN.md).
