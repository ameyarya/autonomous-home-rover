Calibration

1 · Motor directions

Press W. Every wheel should spin forward. If one axle spins backwards, flip its motor.

0 · Steering servo port

Which receiver port the steering servo is plugged into (printed on the board next to the pins). If A/D don't move the servo, this is usually why.

2 · Steering direction

Press A. The front wheels should turn left. If they turn right, swap.

3 · Steering centre

Press STOP (it centres the steering) and look from above. Nudge until the front wheels point straight.

90

4 · Steering range

Press A and D. Raise until the wheels reach nearly full lock; lower it if the servo buzzes or strains at the end.

20

5 · Speed

Motor power for every move (of 2048). Indoor dog-following: slow enough to stop well short of him.
