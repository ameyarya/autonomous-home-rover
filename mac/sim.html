How this works

x runs east, y north, from the plan's bottom-left corner. Rugs and the bedroom carpet are flat (the rover drives over them); everything else blocks it. Doors (red dashed) are no-go lines: draw one across a doorway to keep the rover out; it's invisible to the simulated camera, like a robot vacuum's virtual wall. Every change saves itself to sim/apartment/furniture.json on this Mac (not the public repo) within a second, with a copy in history/ at most once a minute; Rebuild sim makes the simulator use it.
